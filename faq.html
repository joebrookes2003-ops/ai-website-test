Who are Brookes AI courses for?

Brookes AI is for curious beginners and busy professionals who want practical AI skills without the jargon. That includes people who have never used an AI tool, and people who've tried tools like ChatGPT but don't feel they're getting much value from them.

Do I need a technical background?

No. None of our current or planned courses require any coding or technical experience. Everything is explained in plain English, with no assumed knowledge.

What's included when I buy a course?

You get lifetime access to a set of clear, practical lessons that you can work through at your own pace and revisit any time.

How much do the courses cost?

Practical AI for Everyone, our launch course, costs £10. Our two upcoming courses — AI for Work and Build and Launch a Website with AI — are each expected to cost around £30.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. Payments are processed through a trusted, secure payment provider. Brookes AI never sees or stores your card details.

Can I get a refund?

If a course isn't right for you, contact us within 14 days of buying and we'll sort out a refund.

Will there be more courses?

Yes. AI for Work (for managers and professionals) and Build and Launch a Website with AI (for beginners and small-business owners) are both in development. Get in touch if you'd like to be notified when they launch.

How is Brookes AI different from free advice online?

There's a lot of AI content online, but much of it is vague, repetitive, or written by people without a real background in the subject. Brookes AI courses are created by someone with an MSc in Artificial Intelligence, and focused entirely on practical outcomes — saving you time and helping you get better results, rather than generic tips.
